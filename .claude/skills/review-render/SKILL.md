# review-render

1. Open the asset's `assets/previews/<name>/sheet.png` and each individual view you need; open the cited moodboard images.
2. Check: silhouette at 256 px, shape language, palette adherence, detail level vs the flatter board tier, base/origin sanity, scale vs a 0.6 m diorama.
3. For a revision, view old and new sheets side by side and say what changed.
4. For anything going to Siddharth, get the `art-critic` agent's verdict first and include it.
5. Report using his board ids ("closer to #3, still more detailed than #1"). Say what you did not look at.
